Preston Winter Service Catalogue

Winter property maintenance in Preston covers domestic gritting, drainage and exterior repairs for homes across Preston.

Salt Spreading & Gritting

Domestic winter gritting for private driveways, front paths, steps, ramps and entrances. Ask about a one-off visit or planned cover.

Blocked Drains & Drain Repairs

Clearing blocked outdoor drains, gullies and downpipes, and repairing suitable cracked or collapsed drain sections.

Waterlogged Gardens & Surface Drainage

Investigate pooling in gardens and on driveways and patios. Quote for channel drains, land drainage and soakaway work where suitable.

Window, Door & Porch Resealing

Assess draughts and rain getting in around exterior frames. Quote for failed perimeter sealant or worn weather seals where suitable.

Storm, Fence, Gate & Roof Tile Repairs

Assess damaged fencing, gates and slipped or broken tiles. Repair scope and access are confirmed for each job.

Loose, Sunken & Damaged Paving

Repair damaged paths, patios and access paving or relay loose and uneven slabs after assessment.

Winter Garden & Grounds Clearance

Clear leaves and suitable garden debris from home paths, entrances and gardens.

Frost-Damaged Brickwork & Pointing

Assess loose mortar joints, damaged bricks and garden walls and quote for suitable repairs.

Planned Winter Property Checks

Agree checks on drainage, paths, fencing, roof tiles and other exposed areas around your home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GreenFix a gardening company or a construction company?

Both — GreenFix covers grounds and garden care as well as exterior construction and structural repairs, for homes and businesses.

What areas does GreenFix cover?

Preston, Penwortham, Fulwood, Leyland, Cottam, Ingol, Lea, Ashton-on-Ribble, Hutton, Longton and Walton-le-Dale.

Is GreenFix insured?

Yes, GreenFix holds £5M Public Liability Insurance and is a Environment Agency registered Waste Carrier.

How much does a service cost?

Every service has a guide 'from' price on its own page and on the Pricing Guide; the final price depends on the job and is confirmed after a free assessment. Some larger jobs are quotation-only.

Does GreenFix work with businesses as well as homeowners?

Yes — care homes, churches, schools, pubs, restaurants, shops, factories, warehouses, estates and event venues, alongside residential customers.

What happens if I'm not happy with the work?

Contact GreenFix directly (phone, WhatsApp or email) as soon as possible. They respond within 24 hours, inspect the work, and correct it at no extra charge where they're at fault.

How do I book a job or get a quote?

Call or WhatsApp, email, or use the quote form on the homepage — include a preferred date if you'd like a site visit booked in.

What are GreenFix's opening hours?

Monday - Friday, 9:00am - 5:00pm; Saturday, 9:00am - 3:30pm (12:00-1:00pm lunch); Sunday closed.
