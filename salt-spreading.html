Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer salt spreading and gritting in Preston?

Yes. We cover Preston, Penwortham, Fulwood and Leyland for agreed private paths, car parks, entrances and communal access areas. Contact us with your address and treatment needs.

Can I book a one-off visit or regular winter gritting?

You can request a one-off treatment or discuss planned winter visits. We confirm availability, access, timing and the areas to be treated before accepting the work.

How much does salt spreading cost?

We quote individually. The price depends on the size and layout of the site, access, materials and the number of visits. Send photos and approximate dimensions to help us assess the job.

Does gritting guarantee an ice-free surface?

No. Conditions can change after treatment, and rain, traffic or further freezing can mean another visit is needed. Keep monitoring the site and take care on treated surfaces. Repeat treatments need to be agreed.

Do you grit public roads or offer a 24-hour response?

This service is for agreed private property areas. Public road treatment is outside this service. Call to check appointment availability; a 24-hour or automatic weather-triggered response is not included.
