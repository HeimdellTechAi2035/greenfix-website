# GreenFix SEO, GEO and VEO Audit

Date: 2026-06-15

## Executive Summary

GreenFix Exterior Care now has a stronger commercial grounds and exterior maintenance focus across the homepage, metadata, structured data, sitemap, robots file, and AI-readable summary content.

The site is positioned for:

- Commercial grounds maintenance Preston
- Exterior maintenance Lancashire
- Factory and warehouse grounds maintenance
- Care home grounds maintenance
- Church grounds maintenance
- Estate and heritage grounds maintenance
- Venue and event site preparation
- Gutter clearing, jet washing, weed control, fence repairs, bin store cleaning, and site clearances

## SEO Improvements Implemented

- Added a clear homepage summary section explaining what GreenFix does, who it serves, where it works, credentials, services, and contact details.
- Improved homepage title, meta description, Open Graph image, Twitter image, and hero image relevance.
- Added richer `LocalBusiness` schema with stable business ID, logo, image, service type, service topics, payment/currency, and credentials.
- Added `WebPage` schema with primary image, service audience, service area, and service list.
- Kept FAQ schema aligned with visible homepage FAQs.
- Updated sitemap `lastmod` values to 2026-06-15.
- Added image sitemap data for the new commercial hero image.
- Added root-level `llms.txt` for AI agents and generative search systems.
- Updated robots.txt so the sitemap is discoverable and `llms.txt` is crawlable.
- Cleaned older title/meta language that still leaned toward domestic gardening or the previous landlord audience.

## GEO / Generative Engine Optimisation Improvements

AI systems need clear, repeated, consistent facts. The site now states:

- Who GreenFix is
- What GreenFix does
- Who GreenFix serves
- Where GreenFix works
- What services are offered
- What credentials are held
- How to contact the business
- Which pages are authoritative

This information appears in visible copy, JSON-LD, metadata, sitemap signals, and `llms.txt`.

## VEO / Voice and Visual Engine Optimisation Improvements

- Homepage content now answers common voice-style questions directly.
- FAQ questions use natural language suitable for voice search.
- The new hero image better matches the commercial/estate/care/church/venue positioning.
- The homepage image has descriptive alt text and image sitemap metadata.
- Contact details are visible and machine-readable.
