About GreenFix Exterior Care

Property Maintenance & Construction Repairs Since 2015

Who We Are

GreenFix Exterior Care has been serving Preston and Lancashire since 2015. We cover two sides of keeping a property looking good and working properly: regular grounds and garden care, and exterior construction repairs — brickwork, fencing, gates, paving, roofing, drainage, windows and doors. We've completed 500+ jobs and built our reputation on turning up when we say we will and doing the job properly.

What We Cover

Grounds & Garden Care

Lawn mowing, hedge trimming, garden tidy-ups, grounds maintenance for larger sites, pressure washing and window cleaning.

Repairs & Maintenance

Structural and brick repairs, garden wall and fence repairs, paving and patio repairs, roof tile repairs, drainage repairs, and window and door repairs.

Complaints Procedure

We aim to get every job right first time. If something isn't right, here's what happens:

1. Contact us

Call or WhatsApp 07447804597, or email admin@greenfixexterior-care.co.uk, as soon as possible after noticing the issue. Please describe the problem and include photos if you can.

2. We respond within 24 hours

We'll acknowledge your complaint and arrange to inspect the work, in person or from photos.

3. We put it right

Where we're at fault, we return to correct the work at no extra charge, on a timeframe agreed directly with you.

4. If we can't agree a resolution

You can raise the matter with the Citizens Advice consumer service or your local Trading Standards office for independent advice.
