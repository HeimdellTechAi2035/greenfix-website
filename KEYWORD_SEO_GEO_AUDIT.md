## Goal

Position GreenFix Exterior Care as a leading **domestic and commercial** garden and exterior maintenance contractor for Preston, the whole of Lancashire, Wigan, and Greater Manchester.

## Important Warning

Do not repeat keywords unnaturally. Google treats keyword stuffing as spam. Use the target phrase in the title, H1, first paragraph, one subheading where useful, image alt text where relevant, schema/service description, and internal anchor text. Then use natural related phrases.
