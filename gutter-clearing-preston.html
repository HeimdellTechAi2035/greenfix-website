Gutter Clearing FAQs

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Most properties benefit from annual or semi-annual cleaning. Properties near trees may need more frequent cleaning.

What happens if gutters aren't cleaned?

Blocked gutters cause water to overflow, leading to dampness, water damage, and mold. Regular cleaning prevents costly repairs.

Is gutter cleaning dangerous?

Yes—it involves working at height. That's why professional cleaning is safer and more thorough.
