Patio Cleaning FAQs

How much does patio cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on patio size and condition. Call 07447804597 for a free quote with no obligation.

Will jet washing damage my slabs?

Not with the correct technique. We match pressure to surface type — sandstone and natural stone receive lower pressure than concrete slabs.

How do I remove green algae from a patio?

Professional jet washing is the most effective method. It removes algae fully and makes the surface safe again. DIY pressure washers often lack the power for deep cleaning.

Can you clean decking?

Yes — timber and composite decking cleaned carefully to avoid surface damage while removing algae and grime.

Can I combine patio and driveway cleaning?

Yes — combining both in one visit is more cost-effective. We clean both in a single session.

How often should a patio be cleaned?

Once a year works for most patios. Shaded, north-facing, or damp areas may need cleaning every 6–12 months to prevent algae build-up.
