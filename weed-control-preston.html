Weed Control FAQs

What methods do you use for weed control?

How often do I need weed control?

This depends on your garden and the season. Spring and summer see more weed growth. We recommend regular maintenance (fortnightly or monthly) to keep weed problems under control.

Do you offer regular weed control contracts?

Yes! We offer regular monthly or fortnightly weed control services to keep your garden and driveway weed-free all year round.

Can you help with severely overgrown weeds?

Absolutely. No matter how overgrown your garden is, we can handle it. We'll clear the weeds and help establish a regular maintenance schedule to keep it under control.

Is weed control safe for pets and children?

Yes. We prioritize safety and use methods appropriate for families with children and pets. We'll advise you on any precautions needed.
