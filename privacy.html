Privacy Notice

Last updated 30 October 2026

Who we are

GreenFix Exterior Care (Greenfix Exterior Care Ltd, company number 17241761) decides how the personal information described on this page is used. Our address is 11 Lower Croft, Penwortham, Preston PR1 9DJ. Call or WhatsApp 07447 804597 or email admin@greenfixexterior-care.co.uk if you have a privacy question or want to use any of the rights below.

This notice covers the quote form, the monthly garden maintenance sign-up, and messages you send us about a job. The contract terms are on our Terms & Conditions page.

What we collect

On the quote form we collect your name, phone number, property address, a description of the work, a preferred visit date and time of day if you give them, the type of property you tick, and your confirmation that we may use those details to reply.

On the garden maintenance subscription form we collect your first and last name, email address, mobile number, the address where the work will be done, the plan you choose (Lawn, Silver, or Gold), and any preferred visit day or access notes you add. We use the mobile number to call you and confirm the subscription.

If you phone, WhatsApp, or email us, we keep what you send so we can answer and do the work. We do not ask for payment card numbers on this website.

GoCardless

Monthly plans are paid by Direct Debit. You enter bank details on the secure GoCardless page, not on our website. GoCardless is the payment processor for that Direct Debit. We receive what we need to run the plan, including whether the Direct Debit is set up and whether a payment has been collected. We do not store your bank details on this website. GoCardless explains how it handles payment information in its own privacy notice at gocardless.com/privacy.

Why we use it

We use this information to reply to quotes, arrange and carry out visits, take payment, confirm a subscription by phone, and deal with questions or complaints. We do that because you have asked us for a quote or a service, because we need the details to perform that contract (including steps before a contract), and where the law requires us to keep records such as invoices. The confirmation tick on the quote form is your agreement that we can contact you about that enquiry. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as we need it to provide the service you asked for, and afterwards for as long as we need it for tax and accounting and to deal with a complaint or query. We then delete it or remove the details that identify you. We do not keep it for longer than that.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your personal information, to correct it, to delete it, to limit how we use it, or to object to a use that is based on our legitimate interests. Where the law gives you that right, you can ask for a copy in a portable form. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect use that has already happened.

Email admin@greenfixexterior-care.co.uk or call 07447 804597 to use these rights. We will respond within one month. We may need to confirm it is you before we act.

Complaints to the ICO

If you are unhappy with how we have used your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK regulator for personal information, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
