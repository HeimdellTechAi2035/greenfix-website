Health and Safety Policy

Greenfix Exterior Care Ltd

Statement of intent

We will plan and carry out our work to protect the health, safety and welfare of our workers and anyone affected by what we do. This policy covers grounds maintenance, exterior cleaning, painting and decorating, and property repairs carried out by or on behalf of Greenfix Exterior Care Ltd.

We will assess risks before work starts, provide suitable equipment and instruction, consult workers about safety and review incidents and concerns. Anyone working for Greenfix can stop work if conditions become unsafe.

Who is responsible

The director responsible for health and safety will oversee this policy, allocate resources, arrange competent advice where needed and make sure risk assessments, training and equipment checks are carried out. The director will review incidents and follow up corrective actions.

The person leading each job will agree safety arrangements with the site contact, check the work area, brief workers and monitor the agreed controls. If the work or conditions change, they will stop and reassess before restarting.

Everyone working for Greenfix will follow the agreed controls and site rules, use equipment they are competent to operate and report hazards, equipment defects, injuries and near misses promptly. Where we use subcontractors, we will check competence, relevant insurance and their proposed working methods.

Safety arrangements by site

We will agree work areas, access, timing and emergency arrangements with the site contact. We will keep entrances and escape routes clear and pause work if people or animals cannot be kept safely away from the work area.

Veterinary practices

We will coordinate with the practice before starting, keep patient and emergency access clear, and agree how to keep animals and owners away from tools, debris and treated areas. We will follow site hygiene and restricted-access instructions and agree suitable times for noisy work or chemical use. If we encounter sharps or suspected clinical waste, we will stop work in that area and notify practice staff.

Homes and residential properties

We will agree access with the resident or property manager, including how to keep children, household pets and neighbours away from the work area. We will manage trailing leads, hoses, wet surfaces and trip hazards, and check gates, routes and work areas before handing them back.

Warehouses and industrial premises

We will check in with the site contact, follow the site induction and any permit requirements, and agree safe access around forklifts, deliveries and loading bays. We will use designated pedestrian routes and keep work areas clear of moving plant and vehicles. If safe separation cannot be maintained, we will stop and agree a safer time or location with the site contact.

Other occupied premises

At offices, shops, schools, care homes and other public-facing sites, we will agree suitable work times and any safeguarding, access or supervision arrangements with the site manager. We will use barriers, signs or alternative routes where needed to protect staff, customers, visitors and people receiving care.

How we manage work safely

Risk assessment and work planning

Before starting, we will identify the hazards, who could be harmed and what controls are needed. We will brief the people doing the work, provide written assessments to clients on request and prepare a safe system of work or method statement where the risks or the site require one.

Equipment and higher-risk work

We will use suitable equipment, check it before use, maintain it in line with the manufacturer’s instructions and arrange and record further inspections where required. Defective equipment will be removed from use. We will avoid work at height where practical, plan heavy or awkward lifts and provide suitable protective equipment where risks remain. Before work that could disturb building materials, we will check relevant asbestos information and stop if suspect material is found.

Hazardous substances and weed control

We will assess exposure and put suitable controls in place. Where chemical weed control is agreed, we will check that the product is authorised for the intended use and that the operator has suitable training and any required certification. We will follow the product label, agree how to keep people and animals clear and protect drains and watercourses.

Training, lone working and first aid

We will assign work according to competence and record relevant training. Where lone working is suitable, we will agree how the worker checks in and gets help. Before work starts, we will confirm first aid and emergency arrangements. We will record and review injuries, near misses and work-related ill health and notify the appropriate enforcing authority where reporting is required.

Review and contact

The responsible director will share this policy with workers and review it at least annually and after a significant incident or change in work, equipment or working arrangements.
