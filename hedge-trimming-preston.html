Hedge Trimming FAQs

How often should hedges be trimmed?

Most hedges benefit from trimming once or twice per year during growing season (typically spring and summer). We'll recommend the best schedule for your hedge type.

Do you handle large overgrown hedges?

Yes! We can handle hedges of any size, including severely overgrown ones. We'll restore them to shape and establish a regular maintenance schedule.

Do you remove hedge clippings?

Yes. We remove all hedge clippings and debris from your property, leaving your garden clean and tidy.

Do you offer seasonal hedge trimming packages?

Yes! We offer spring and summer trimming packages to keep your hedges looking fantastic throughout the year. Regular maintenance prevents overgrowth.

What types of hedges do you trim?

We trim all hedge types: hawthorn, privet, yew, holly, beech, laurel, and ornamental shrubs. Whatever you have, we can maintain it.

How much does hedge trimming cost?

Pricing depends on the size and type of your hedges and the complexity of the work. We offer competitive rates for both one-off jobs and regular maintenance. Contact us for a free quote.
