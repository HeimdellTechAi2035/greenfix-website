Frequently Asked Questions

What storm damage can GreenFix repair?

We assess damaged fencing, gates, roof tiles and suitable exterior masonry or property repairs. The work we can accept depends on the damage, safe access and whether specialist assessment is needed.

Can you repair a fence that has blown down?

Yes. We can assess panels, posts and fixings and quote for repair or replacement. Photos taken from a safe location help us understand how much of the fence is affected.

Can you attend immediately after a storm?

Call to check current availability. We confirm a realistic appointment once we understand the damage, access and weather conditions. Same-day attendance and 24-hour emergency cover are not guaranteed.

How much do storm damage repairs cost?

We provide a quote for the specific damage. Materials, labour, access equipment and waste removal depend on the job. Any scaffolding or specialist requirements are discussed before you approve the work.

What should I send when requesting a quote?

Send your name, phone number, property address, a description of the damage and photos taken without approaching unsafe areas. For a rental property, include the person who can arrange access and who will approve the work.

Can I use your quote for an insurance enquiry?

You can share our repair quote with your insurer. Check their approval requirements before authorising work; cover and claim decisions are made by your insurer.
