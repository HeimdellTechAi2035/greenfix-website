How we handle personal information is explained in our Privacy Notice.

Quotes and guide prices

Prices published on this website are guide prices only. They are not a fixed quote. We confirm the price after a free assessment of the actual job, and before work starts. The confirmed price depends on the size of the garden or the repair, its condition, access, and what you want done.

Some jobs are quotation only. We tell you the price we can do that work for, and we do not start it until you have agreed.

Monthly garden maintenance plans

You can pay monthly for regular garden maintenance. All three plans are fortnightly visits. The payment is monthly, by Direct Debit via GoCardless.

Lawn Package, £39 per month. Fortnightly visits for lawn mowing and edging, with grass cuttings cleared and removed and lawn edges tidied.

Silver Package, £59 per month. Fortnightly visits. Everything in the Lawn Package, plus hedge trimming (up to 3 hedges) and tidy garden borders.

Gold Package, £79 per month. Fortnightly visits. Everything in the Silver Package, plus weeding of beds and borders and priority scheduling.

After you choose a plan, you set up the Direct Debit on the secure GoCardless page. We will call you to confirm the subscription before visits start. That confirmation call is part of setting up the Direct Debit agreement. Direct Debit payments are covered by the Direct Debit Guarantee.

You can cancel any time. There is no notice period. Contact us by phone or email, or cancel the Direct Debit with your bank. We will not take further monthly payments after you cancel. Visits that have already been carried out stay charged.

If you signed up online or by phone, you have a 14-day cooling-off period for the subscription under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013. You can cancel in those 14 days for any reason. If no visit has taken place, you will not be charged for the plan. If you asked us to start during the cooling-off period and a visit has already been done, you pay for the work carried out.

Plan details and sign-up are on the garden maintenance subscription page.

Access to the property

For a regular garden visit you do not need to be at home, as long as we can reach the garden safely. Tell us about gates, codes, dogs, or anything else we need, when you book or on the sign-up form. If we cannot get safe access on the day, we will contact you and rearrange the visit. We will not go into a garden or property where it is not safe to do so.

Weather and rescheduling

The work is outdoors. If rain, wind, frost, or waterlogged ground would make a visit unsafe, or would spoil the work, we will contact you and move it to the next suitable day. You can also ask us to move a visit. Moving a visit is not a cancellation of a monthly plan.

Payment for one-off jobs

A one-off job is charged at the price we confirm with you after the free assessment, not at the guide price on its own. We invoice that agreed price. Monthly maintenance plans are paid by the monthly Direct Debit, rather than a separate invoice for each visit. For regular commercial work we can invoice and agree an account arrangement.

Liability

We carry out the work with reasonable care and skill. Our liability to you is limited to the extent permitted by law. Nothing in these terms limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or any other liability that cannot legally be limited.

Complaints

If something is not right, contact us as soon as you can by phone, WhatsApp, or email, and tell us what happened. We aim to respond within 24 hours, look at the work, and, where we are at fault, put it right at no extra charge. The steps are set out in our complaints procedure. If we cannot agree a resolution, you can ask Citizens Advice or your local Trading Standards office for independent advice.
